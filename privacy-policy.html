Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Mattermail ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the highest level of security for your professional communications. This Privacy Policy comprehensively explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard your information when you install and use the Mattermail Google Workspace Add-on (the "Service").

Mattermail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Access and Process

Mattermail operates on a strict "least privilege" architecture. We only request and access the precise data scopes necessary to facilitate the transfer of information between your Google Workspace and your authorized Clio account.

A. Google Workspace Data

  • Active Email Content: We only access the specific, currently active email thread (including metadata like Sender, Recipient, Subject, Timestamp, Body Content, and Attachments) that you have explicitly opened and chosen to file.
  • Primary Email Address: We access your authenticated Google account email address solely to power our automated "Safety Net" protocol, which alerts you via email if a background synchronization job fails.

B. Clio Manage Data

  • Contextual Fetching: To render the Add-on UI, we temporarily fetch your open matters, task types, and firm users from your Clio account. This allows you to select the correct filing destinations.
  • Filing Submissions: When you execute a save action, we transmit the selected email data to create Communications, Task Entries, Time Logs, and Document Uploads in your Clio workspace.

C. Analytics and System Logs

  • Usage Metrics: We utilize a secure, anonymized Firebase database to track high-level Daily Active User (DAU) metrics. This is limited to pinging when the application is opened to monitor overall system health and server loads.

2. Information We Explicitly Do Not Collect

To ensure total compliance with legal privilege and confidentiality standards, we enforce strict data boundaries:

  • No Global Inbox Access: We do not read, scan, index, or access your inbox history, spam folders, or any email threads outside of the specific message you are actively viewing in the Add-on sidebar.
  • No Persistent Storage of Client Data: We do not store your email content, attachment files, or Clio matter details on any external or proprietary servers. The data flows directly from Google's servers to Clio's servers via secure API requests.
  • No Credential Harvesting: We do not capture, see, or store your Clio account passwords. All authentication is handled through standardized, secure OAuth 2.0 protocols.

3. Data Retention and Temporary Background Processing

Because we do not maintain a centralized database for your communications, there is no personal or client data for us to retain long-term.

  • Ephemeral Queues: If you elect to save a large email thread or multiple heavy attachments, the data payload is temporarily held in your own Google Workspace's secure Apps Script Property Store for approximately 1 to 2 minutes while the background worker processes the upload.
  • Immediate Destruction: The moment the synchronization to Clio either successfully completes or registers a terminal failure, this temporary data packet is permanently and irreversibly deleted from the queue.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Disclosures

We absolutely do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data, email content, or usage metrics to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Your data is only transmitted to third-party services under the following specific conditions:

  • To Clio Manage: Information is transmitted to Clio solely when you explicitly click the "Save to Clio" (or equivalent) button to fulfill your requested workflow.
  • To Google LLC: As the Service is hosted on Google's Apps Script infrastructure, data processing inherently occurs within Google's secure cloud environments, subject to Google's enterprise security standards.

5. Security Measures

We implement robust, industry-standard technical measures to protect your data during transit:

  • All data transfers between Google, the Mattermail Service, and Clio occur over heavily encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • API tokens and authentication keys are securely stored within Google's encrypted credential management systems, inaccessible to the public or external systems.

6. Your Rights and Revoking Access

You maintain absolute control over the Service's access to your accounts. You may instantly revoke Mattermail's permissions at any time without prior notice by:

  • Navigating to your Google Account Security Settings and removing Mattermail from your "Third-party apps with account access" list.
  • Navigating to your Clio Manage Application settings and deauthorizing the Mattermail integration.

Revoking access will immediately sever the Service's ability to read your active emails or push data to Clio.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy as our Service evolves or as required by changing regulatory standards. If significant changes are made concerning how we handle your data, we will attempt to notify you via the email address associated with your account or through a prominent notice within the Add-on UI. Continued use of the Service following any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, our data handling practices, or your compliance requirements, please contact the developer, Manya Verma, directly by submitting an inquiry through our Feedback & Support Form.
