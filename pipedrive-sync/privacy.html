Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Pipedrive Gmail Add-on (the "Application") developed by Manya Verma ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)
The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Application, we access specific data to provide our core CRM integration features:

  • Gmail Data: The Application reads the contents, sender, and recipient details of the specific email thread you currently have open in order to log it to Pipedrive.
  • Pipedrive Data: The Application accesses your Pipedrive CRM to retrieve contacts, deals, organizations, and to create notes or activities based on your direct input.
  • User Profile: We collect your basic Google account email address to verify your identity and manage your session state.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide the features of the Application. Specifically:

  • To sync the currently viewed email to your Pipedrive CRM when you explicitly click "Log Email" or "Save."
  • To display relevant Pipedrive contact data inside your Gmail sidebar.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes. We do not use human review of your email data unless strictly necessary for security purposes or required by law.

3. Data Storage and Retention

The Application acts primarily as a bridge between Gmail and Pipedrive. We temporarily cache standard, non-sensitive CRM identifiers (like a list of deal names or contact IDs) in Google Apps Script Cache Services to ensure the Application runs quickly. This cache automatically expires and is routinely wiped. Your actual email bodies are routed directly to Pipedrive via secure API and are not stored on our own external servers.

4. Third-Party Access

The Application securely transfers data only to Pipedrive (via official API integrations) at your direct request. We do not share data with any other external third parties.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security page and removing access for this add-on.
