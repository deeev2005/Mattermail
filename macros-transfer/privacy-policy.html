Privacy Policy

Product: Macros Transfer (Google Sheets add-on)  |  Effective date: October 16, 2026  |  Last updated: October 16, 2026
Macros Transfer helps you convert Excel VBA macros into Google Apps Script. To do this, it processes your Google account details, the workbook files or macro code you submit, and files created in your Google Drive. This policy explains what is collected, why, how your data is protected, and the choices you have.

1. Who We Are

Macros Transfer ("we", "us", "our", the "Service") is operated by Deepesh Verma, located in Madhya Pradesh, India. You can contact us at automatic.self.attendance@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information (from your Google account)

2.2 Usage and account records

  • Timestamps: First installed and last active dates.
  • Usage metrics: Conversion count counter to manage quota and plan tiers.
  • Subscription tier: Plan status (e.g., free or premium).
  • Billing customer ID: If paid plans are enabled, payment customer tokens handled securely by a PCI-compliant payment processor. We never store credit card or payment account numbers.

2.3 Content you submit

  • Excel workbooks: Supported workbooks (.xlsm, .xls, .xlsb, .xltm; up to 25 MB each) that you upload for conversion.
  • VBA macro code: Code snippets that you paste directly or that are parsed from your workbook.
  • Generated output: The converted Google Apps Script code and migration report generated for you.

Your workbook may contain business or personal data in its spreadsheet cells. Macro processing only analyzes macro code; cell data is not extracted or read for code translation. When converting full workbooks, the spreadsheet is uploaded directly to your own Google Drive using your authorization.

2.4 Technical & Operational Data

  • Operational logs: Temporary server diagnostic logs (such as timestamps, response status codes, and non-identifying operational metrics) are maintained to ensure system reliability and troubleshoot errors. These logs do not retain file contents or spreadsheet cell data.
  • Network data: Standard network metadata (such as IP address) is processed in transit by our secure cloud hosting providers and Google when requests are routed.
  • Fonts: The add-on sidebar loads standard typography from Google Fonts, which processes standard browser network headers.

4. How Your Data Flows Through the Service

  1. Session Authentication: When you launch the add-on, your basic Google identity (email and name) is verified to initialize your workspace session and check plan limits.
  2. Transient Code Extraction: For uploaded workbooks or pasted code, macro code is processed securely in volatile memory over encrypted HTTPS. Files and code are processed ephemerally and are not saved to persistent disk or retained on our processing servers after the request finishes.
  3. AI Code Translation: Macro logic (and never your spreadsheet cell data) is converted into Google Apps Script code using enterprise-grade AI APIs under strict data privacy controls.
  4. Direct Storage in Your Google Drive: The resulting Google Sheet, converted Apps Script code, and compatibility report are saved directly to your personal Google Drive under your authorization. We do not store or keep copies of your converted spreadsheets or script files.

The resulting spreadsheets and scripts remain entirely in your Google account.
